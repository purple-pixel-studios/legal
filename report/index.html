Report content

Zonu AI · draft 2 · 2026-11-10

Use this form to tell us about a character's replies that are inaccurate, offensive, sexual, about self-harm, or that impersonate a real person, or about anything else you think we should look at. If you use the app, you can also report a single reply there, from the reply's action row or by long-pressing it. Rights-holders and authorities should use the contact page.

What happens to a report

  • A report goes to the developer, who reads it. We aim to look at reports about self-harm, sexual content, impersonation and offensive content within 24 hours, and reports about inaccuracy and other topics within 72 hours. These are targets, not guarantees, and the service is run by one person.
  • A daily summary and alerts when a character collects many reports go to the developer by e-mail. The summary holds counts, character ids, categories and report ids only, never your note.
  • Reports are used to improve the characters and the safety filters. We do not promise that a reply is removed, or that a character is changed or taken down, because of a report. A report never suspends a character automatically.
  • If you leave a contact, we may write to you when we need more details or once we have acted on your report. We do not reply to every report.
  • A report, with its optional note and contact, is kept 30 days, or 90 days if we are investigating it, and then deleted. A reply you reported from the app, and the question it answers, is kept for the same period even if you delete your account; your note is deleted with the account.

Do not put message text you want kept private, or details about other people, in the note. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number; this form is not monitored round the clock.
