Privacy policy

Closed test build · effective 2026-10-29

This notice covers the closed test of the app on Google Play. It describes what the test build actually does. A fuller notice, in every language the app supports, replaces it before the public launch.

Who is responsible

An individual developer publishes the app. Contact: purplepixxelstudios@gmail.com.

What the app collects

  • A device identity. On first launch the app creates a random device ID and a random secret. Our server stores the device ID, a one-way hash of the secret (never the secret itself), an internal record number we assign to the device, the platform (Android), your time zone, and when the device first registered and last connected. It may also store a two-letter store country hint, read from your Google Play account through the subscription SDK and sent when the device renews its access; it is used to switch rewarded ads off or shrink their grant in some countries and, for a Free user only and without any owner or device ID, our server also records it as the country of aggregated daily usage-cost statistics. The hint is deleted with the device record; the daily aggregate carries no identifier and is kept for cost statistics. There is no name, e-mail address or phone number; Google sign-in is optional and, if used, adds only your Google account identifier.
  • Your chats. The messages you send and the replies you receive are stored on our server so that your conversations work, together with when each message was sent, which character, card version and model answered, and the number of tokens used.
  • What the characters remember. Unless you switch it off or use a temporary chat, our server derives short facts about you (for example interests, background, preferences and goals) and conversation summaries from your messages, and stores them, linked to the message they came from and, unless you let facts be shared across characters, to the character. The instructions we give the model tell it to skip sensitive topics such as health, religion or politics, and a filter drops facts the model itself labels sensitive; both rely on the model, so we cannot promise that nothing sensitive is ever kept. The facts are used only to make later replies more personal: they are added to the character's prompt, either for that character alone or, if you choose "Shared across characters", for all of them. In the app under Settings → Memory you can switch memory off, choose separate or shared memory, delete single facts and delete everything remembered. Deleting a chat removes what was learned from it, and deleting your account removes all of it.
  • A keyed hash of your IP address and a per-device counter, used only to limit how many new identities one network can create and how often one device can renew its access (abuse prevention). We store a keyed hash, not the address. Counters stop being used once they are more than two days old, and are deleted the next time any device registers or renews its access.
  • Service logs that record errors and request status. Our own logs never contain message text. The infrastructure logs of Supabase and Cloudflare record your IP address and request details for a short period (about one day on our Supabase plan) before they are discarded.

How it is used

We use this data only to run the chat service, to keep it secure, and to prevent abuse. Your messages are never used to train AI models. The build of this version contains an anonymous-statistics SDK (PostHog), which sends nothing in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland until you tap Allow, and is on by default elsewhere with a switch in Settings; an ads SDK (Google AdMob), which starts when the daily-limit screen offers an eligible Free user an optional rewarded video (from the fourth day of use, never to Plus) and, when Google's consent platform allows ad requests, loads a video before any tap; and a subscription SDK (RevenueCat) for Plus purchases through Google Play. Google sign-in is optional and, if you use it, we keep only your Google account identifier. We do not use your messages for advertising. The full description is on the privacy policy page (docs/legal/site/privacy/, in every supported language) that replaces this notice.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase hosts the database in the EU (Frankfurt) and runs the server functions on its infrastructure, which may be outside the EU. The Cloudflare network routes requests to it, and in doing so it handles your IP address.
  • Google (Gemini API) generates the character replies and, when memory is on, derives the remembered facts and summaries from your messages. Each message is sent together with up to the eight most recent earlier messages from the same conversation, the language the app is set to, and the character's instructions. Google may process them outside the EU. We use Google's paid API tier, whose terms say Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products.
  • Google (sign-in), only if you choose Google sign-in, identifies your Google account to us. The app passes us Google's sign-in token, which can include your name and e-mail address; our server checks it, reads only the Google account identifier and stores nothing else from it.
  • Google Play distributes the test build, gives us aggregated install and crash statistics for it, and handles Plus payments.
  • RevenueCat manages Plus subscriptions: it receives your random user number and the purchase details Google Play reports (product, price, store, billing country, transaction and subscription state), never messages. Our server also keeps whether the free trial was used and RevenueCat's user number.
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent platform serve and gate the optional rewarded video; the Google ads SDK reads device and app information, including the advertising ID.
  • PostHog (EU cloud) receives anonymous usage statistics where you allow them or your region permits them; events hold fixed values only, never messages.

On your device

The app keeps a local copy of your chats and your device secret in app-private storage, which is excluded from device backups. "Clear local data" in Settings deletes both from the device. It does not delete the copy on our server; see below.

How long we keep it

We keep closed-test data only for as long as the closed test runs. We delete all of it no later than 30 days after the test ends, or sooner if you ask. Separately, data of a user none of whose devices has connected to our server for 12 months is deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

E-mail purplepixxelstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Include the Device ID shown in the app under Settings → Device ID. If you signed in with Google and no longer have the app, send a Device ID you noted earlier, or reinstall the app, sign in with Google and read the Device ID under Settings → Device ID; we handle each request by hand. We delete data only when the Device ID matches a device that still exists; if we cannot verify the request, we delete nothing and say so, and data of a user none of whose devices has connected to our server for 12 months is deleted automatically. Each use of "Clear local data" creates a new Device ID, so send each one you know. We act within 30 days and reply by e-mail. If you have the app, you can delete in the app instead: Settings → Delete account and data. Our server then deletes your chats (they are marked deleted at once and the messages are erased by an hourly job, normally within a few hours), everything the characters remember about you, your usage counters and subscription record, and the link to your Google account, and it retires your device; the app then wipes its local copy. Messages attached to a report you sent may be kept for a limited review period. If the app cannot reach our server, nothing is deleted and you can try again. Deleting your account in the app does not delete your Google account.

Children

The app is for ages 18 and over and has no mode for younger people. If you believe a minor is using it, write to the contact address above and we will delete the data we can link to them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data or its deletion, and you can object to its processing, by writing to the contact address above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
